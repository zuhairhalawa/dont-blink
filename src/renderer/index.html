DON'T BLINK IT ONLY MOVES WHEN YOUR EYES CLOSE

Before the lights go out

  • The camera watches your eyes. The game uses your webcam to see when you blink or look away, and to read your pulse and breathing rate.
  • Video stays on this computer. The Presage SmartSpectra SDK processes the video on this device, and the head check that notices when you turn away (Google MediaPipe) runs on this device too. Frames are never uploaded or saved. The SDK goes online only to check the API key.
  • One photo, kept only in memory. When the Watcher gets closest, the game takes one still of your face for the end card. It and your blinks and pulse stay in memory for the round and are gone when you close the game. Nothing is saved unless you choose Save image. To keep your face out of a recording or stream, turn on Hide my face on the next screen.
  • A game, not a medical device. The numbers are estimates for fun. Expect flickering light and one sudden loud sound.

Starting the camera…

Reading your pulse. This takes 20 to 40 seconds.

  1. Every time you blink, it moves closer.
  2. Look away and it moves twice.
  3. Hold your stare for 8 seconds and it backs off.

It sees full blinks. A quick flutter may slip past.
